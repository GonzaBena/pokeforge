**Ghost-type Pokémon** have fascinated trainers ever since Generation I in Red & Blue. Cloaked in folklore, urban legends, and chilling Pokédex descriptions, they remain the most enigmatic creatures in the Pokémon universe.

---

## 1. The Gengar & Clefable Connection

One of the most famous theories is the uncanny morphological connection between **Gengar** and **Clefable**:

* Their body silhouettes, ear shapes, and stances are almost identical.
* Clefable was originally pure Normal-type, while Gengar is Ghost/Poison: spectral counterparts that cannot hit each other with standard Normal attacks.
* Is Gengar literally the departed shadow or ghost of a Clefable?

> "Should you feel that you have been suddenly hit by a chilliness, it is evidence of an approaching Gengar. There is no escaping it." — Pokédex

---

## 2. Gastly and Cloyster's Shell

Looking back at Gastly's original Gen I sprite and Cloyster's interior shell face, their resemblance is striking: both feature a dark shadowy orb with intense gleaming eyes and a sly grin. Fan theories suggest that Gastly is the disembodied spirit of a Cloyster that lost its shell.

---

## 3. Lavender Town and the Pokémon Tower

Lavender Town introduced the themes of loss and resting spirits into Pokémon games. The ghost of mother **Marowak** protecting the tower until calmed by the Silph Scope remains one of the franchise's most iconic storylines.

### Quick Facts:
1. **Dual Immunities**: Ghost is the only elemental type with **two inherent complete immunities** (Normal and Fighting).
2. **Untrappable**: Modern mechanics guarantee that Ghost-type Pokémon cannot be trapped by abilities like *Shadow Tag* or moves like *Mean Look*.
3. **Mimikyu's True Face**: Pokédex entries state that a researcher who peered under Mimikyu's disguise died of shock.
