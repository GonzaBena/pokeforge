The official Pokémon Doubles format (**VGC**) is fast-paced and intensely tactical. Unlike singles matches where passive switches are frequent, every single turn in doubles can make or break the outcome.

Here are the four pillars of successful competitive team composition.

---

## 1. Speed Control

He who moves first dictates the match:

* **Tailwind**: Doubles your party's speed for 4 turns (e.g., Tornadus, Whimsicott).
* **Trick Room**: Inverts turn order for 5 turns, empowering bulky, slow powerhouses to strike first (e.g., Dusclops, Hatterene).
* **Stat drops & paralysis**: *Thunder Wave*, *Icy Wind*, or *Electroweb* to damage opponents while punishing their speed tiers.

---

## 2. Redirection & Disruption

Shield your primary win conditions while they set up or execute game-winning blows:

* **Follow Me / Rage Powder**: Forces single-target moves onto your supportive tank (e.g., Clefairy, Amoonguss).
* **Fake Out**: Flinches a target on turn 1, preventing dangerous attacks or setups.

> "The hardest-hitting sweeper in the world is useless if it faints before ever clicking an attack."

---

## 3. Positioning with Pivots

Gaining positional advantage without sacrificing tempo:

* **U-turn & Volt Switch**: Deal chip damage while rotating in the optimal teammate to counter your opponent's play.
* **Intimidate**: Softens physical damage across both opposing monsters on entrance (e.g., Incineroar, Landorus-T).
